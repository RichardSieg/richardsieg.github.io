👋 Hi, I'm Richard! I am a full-time professor for NLP and LLMs at the <a href="https://www.th-koeln.de/" target="_blank">Technische Hochschule Köln</a>. My [university webpage](https://www.th-koeln.de/personen/richard.sieg/).

👨‍🔬 My research interests are the extraction of structured data from documents and text simplification.

👨‍💻 I have a background in Discrete Mathematics and received my PhD at the University of Osnabrück in Combinatorial Algebra.

📻 Before becoming a professor, I worked as an NLP and ML Engineer at the Cologne Start-Up <a href="https://www.how.fm/" target="_blank">how.fm</a>.
